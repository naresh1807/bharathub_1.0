1. Information We Collect

Personal Information: When you register on BharatHub, we collect your name, email address, mobile number, date of birth, gender, and profile photograph.

Professional Information: Academic qualifications, work experience, skills, certifications, resume/CV, cover letters, and job preferences.

Company Information (Employers): Company name, CIN, GSTIN, PAN, registered address, branch locations, HR contact details, and corporate email.

Vendor Information: Business name, GST/PAN number, shop address, product/service catalog, shop front photographs, and business email.

Usage Data: Pages visited, search queries, job applications, time spent on the platform, device information, IP address, and browser type.

Communication Data: Messages sent through our Chat and Mail Gateway system between users on the platform.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To create and manage your BharatHub account with a unique ID (BH/EMP/VND)
  • To match job seekers with relevant job opportunities using AI-powered algorithms
  • To display employer profiles to relevant candidates and vice versa
  • To facilitate B2B commerce between verified vendors and employers
  • To send OTP verification messages and account security alerts
  • To send job alerts, application status updates, and platform notifications
  • To improve our platform features and user experience
  • To comply with legal obligations under Indian law
  • To prevent fraud, abuse, and unauthorized access

3. Information Sharing & Disclosure

With Employers: When you apply for a job, your profile information including name, skills, qualifications, and contact details are shared with the respective employer.

With Vendors: Employer contact details may be shared with verified vendors for B2B transaction purposes when an order is placed.

We do NOT:

  • Sell your personal data to third parties for advertising purposes
  • Share your data with companies outside the BharatHub ecosystem without your consent
  • Use your data for any purpose other than what is described in this policy

Legal Disclosure: We may disclose your information if required by Indian law, court order, or government authority.

4. Data Security

BharatHub implements enterprise-grade security measures to protect your data:

  • All passwords are encrypted using bcrypt hashing — we never store plain-text passwords
  • All data transmission is secured with TLS/SSL encryption (HTTPS)
  • Two-Factor Authentication (2FA) via OTP is mandatory for all account logins
  • Regular security audits and vulnerability assessments
  • Unique BharatHub IDs (BH2026XXXX) add an additional layer of identity verification
  • Session management with HttpOnly cookies to prevent XSS attacks

Despite these measures, no internet transmission is 100% secure. We encourage you to use strong passwords and never share your OTP with anyone.

5. Cookies & Tracking Technologies

BharatHub uses the following types of cookies:

  • Essential Cookies: Required for login sessions, security, and basic platform functionality. These cannot be disabled.
  • Preference Cookies: Remember your language, display preferences, and dashboard settings.
  • Analytics Cookies: Help us understand how users navigate BharatHub to improve the platform (anonymized data only).

We do NOT use advertising or tracking cookies. You can manage cookie preferences in your browser settings.

6. Your Rights & Choices

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (India), you have the following rights:

  • Right to Access: Request a copy of all personal data we hold about you
  • Right to Correction: Update or correct inaccurate personal information
  • Right to Erasure: Request deletion of your account and associated data
  • Right to Grievance: Lodge a complaint about data handling
  • Right to Withdraw Consent: Withdraw consent for data processing (may affect platform functionality)

To exercise these rights, contact our Data Protection Officer at privacy@bharathub.in

7. Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account:

  • Personal profile data is deleted within 30 days
  • Job application records are retained for 90 days for dispute resolution
  • Financial transaction records are retained for 7 years as required by Indian tax law
  • Communication logs are deleted within 60 days

8. Third-Party Services

BharatHub may use the following third-party services:

  • Email OTP Service: For sending verification OTPs to your email
  • SMS Gateway: For sending mobile OTPs
  • Payment Gateway: For processing B2B marketplace transactions (PCI-DSS compliant)
  • Cloud Storage: For storing uploaded documents and profile photos securely

Each third-party service provider is bound by data processing agreements ensuring compliance with Indian data protection laws.

9. Children's Privacy

BharatHub is intended for users who are 18 years of age or older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from minors. If we discover that a user under 18 has registered, we will immediately delete their account and data.
