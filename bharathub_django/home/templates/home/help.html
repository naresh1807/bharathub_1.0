👤 Account & Login

How do I register on BharatHub?▼
Click on "Register Free" on the Home page. Select your role (Employee, Employer, or Vendor) and complete the 4-step registration form. You will need to verify your email with a 6-digit OTP to complete registration.
What is a BharatHub ID?▼
After successful registration, you receive a unique BharatHub ID: BHEMP26070001234 for employees, BHEMR26070001234 for employers, and BHVND26070001234 for vendors (prefix + year + month + a unique number). This ID is permanent and is used for login and identification on the platform.
Why do I need OTP every time I login?▼
BharatHub uses mandatory Two-Factor Authentication (2FA) for all logins to protect your account. When you click Login, an OTP is automatically sent to your registered email or mobile. This ensures only you can access your account, even if your password is compromised.
I didn't receive the OTP. What should I do?▼
1. Check your spam/junk folder. 2. Wait 2 minutes and try Resend OTP. 3. Ensure the email address you entered matches your registered email. 4. If the problem persists, contact support@bharathub.in with your BharatHub ID.
How do I reset my forgotten password?▼
Click "Forgot Password?" on the login page. Enter your registered Email, Mobile number, and BharatHub ID for verification. An OTP will be sent to your email. After verification, you can set a new password.
Can I change my registered email address?▼
Yes. Go to Profile → Security Settings → Change Email. You will need to verify both your old and new email addresses with OTPs to complete the change.

💼 Jobs & Applications

How does job matching work on BharatHub?▼
BharatHub uses AI-powered skill matching. When employers post jobs with required skills, our algorithm matches them with candidates who have listed those skills on their profiles. Jobs are displayed in the candidate dashboard based on match percentage.
How do I apply for a job?▼
Browse jobs in your dashboard and click on any job card to view full details. Click "Apply Now" to open the application form. Fill in your details, upload your resume (PDF/DOC, max 5MB), write a cover letter, and submit. Your application status will be tracked in "Applied Status" tab.
Can I apply for the same job twice?▼
No. Once you apply for a job, the Apply button changes to "Applied" and you cannot submit another application for the same position. If you need to update your application, contact the employer directly through the Chat system.
How do I track my job application status?▼
Go to your Candidate Dashboard → "Applied Status" tab. You will see all your applications with their current status: Applied, Under Review, Shortlisted, Interview Scheduled, or Rejected. You can also contact the employer directly through the Chat Gateway.
Is it free to apply for jobs on BharatHub?▼
Yes, applying for jobs on BharatHub is completely free for candidates. BharatHub's Terms of Service prohibit employers from charging candidates any fees for applying or interviewing. If any employer asks you to pay, report it immediately to support@bharathub.in.

🛍️ B2B Marketplace & Vendors

How do I purchase from the B2B Marketplace?▼
Only verified employers can access the B2B Marketplace. Go to Employer Dashboard → Shopping. Browse products by category, set quantity, and click "Add to Cart" or "Buy Now". Review your cart and proceed to checkout. Your order will be tracked in "My Orders".
How do I track my B2B order?▼
After checkout, go to Employer Dashboard → My Orders. You will see all your orders with a 4-step status tracker: Order Placed → Vendor Confirmed → Shipped → Delivered. You can also cancel orders or reorder from the same page.
How do I register as a vendor on BharatHub?▼
Click "Register your Business" on the Vendor Login page. Complete the 4-step vendor registration: Business Details → Location & Shop Photo (mandatory) → Product Catalog → Security Verification. You must verify both your email and mobile OTPs to complete registration.
How long does vendor verification take?▼
After submitting your registration, our team reviews your GST/PAN documents and shop front photo within 2-3 business days. You will receive an email confirmation once your vendor account is approved and your products go live on the marketplace.
